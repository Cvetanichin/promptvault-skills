name: rapid-evidence-review-prompt
description: "Background research during proposal development or evaluation prep."
---

Conduct a rapid evidence review on [TOPIC] with focus on [REGION/COUNTRY]. Include: (1) Definition and scope, (2) Key statistics (with sources), (3) Policy and legal framework, (4) Civil society response, (5) Gaps and research needs. Max 800 words. Flag findings older than 3 years.

When invoked, ask the user to specify: [REGION/COUNTRY], [TOPIC] before proceeding. If the user already supplied this information in their request, use it directly instead of asking.
