name: requirements-clarification-protocol
description: "Tackling unfamiliar or underspecified tasks."
---

Task: [TASK DESCRIPTION]. Before I begin, I clarify requirements:
- Who is the target audience?
- What is the expected format and length?
- What constraints apply?
- What does 'done' look like?
- What is the most common failure mode?
After answering, state clarified understanding and complete the task.

When invoked, ask the user to specify: [TASK DESCRIPTION] before proceeding. If the user already supplied this information in their request, use it directly instead of asking.
